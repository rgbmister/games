Biplane— vs —Zeppelin

Destroy the giant zeppelin while surviving its escort fighters.
Use the mouse to aim and the keyboard to fly. Her engines glow when your reticle is over them — shoot them out to slow her. Clouds hide you. Do not ram the zeppelin.
  • Mouse: aim / steer
  • Click game area to lock mouse
  • W / S: throttle
  • A / D or Arrow keys: turn / pitch
  • Left click / Space: shoot
  • M: sound on/off
